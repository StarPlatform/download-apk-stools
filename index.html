Account and security

What you should know

Short and clear.

A STools-only account

You need to create an account to use the app. We do not ask for a phone number, ID card, or links to social media accounts.

The app does not run silently

STools does not run in the background without your knowledge.

Check the APK yourself

Upload the APK file to VirusTotal.com before installing. After installation, open Play Store, tap your profile, choose Play Protect, then tap Scan.

Q&A

Frequently asked questions

Common questions and answers.

Why do I need to log in?

So that the attendance, finance, and grade data you save stays separate from other users and is synced to your account.

What if I forget my password?

Contact us by email and include your account username.

Is personal data required to sign up?

No. The account is used only within STools, without a phone number, ID card, or social media account.

Can STools be used on Windows?

Yes. Download the installer for Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and install it like any other program. The app requires an internet connection.

Why does Windows show a warning during installation?

This is Windows SmartScreen, a built-in check for newly released apps that few people have downloaded yet. Click More info, then Run anyway to continue.

Having trouble installing, or found a bug?

Send an email to startoolsid@gmail.com and mention your device type and the problem.
